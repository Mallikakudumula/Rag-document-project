# Acme Retail IT Security Policy

## Passwords and access
Passwords must be at least 12 characters long. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, VPN and all cloud systems. Passwords must be changed every 90 days and must never be shared.

## Devices
All company laptops use full-disk encryption. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT helpdesk immediately. Personal USB drives are not allowed on company laptops.

## Phishing
Report any suspected phishing email to security@acme.example within 1 hour. Do not click links or open attachments from unknown senders.
