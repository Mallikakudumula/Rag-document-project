Acme Retail Employee Expense Policy

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase date, with original receipts attached.

Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to Rs. 1,500 per day. Hotel stays must be booked through the approved travel portal.

Any single expense above Rs. 10,000 needs approval from both the line manager and the finance team before purchase. Alcohol is never reimbursable.

Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly salary run.
