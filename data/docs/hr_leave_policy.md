# Acme Retail HR Leave Policy

## Annual leave
Every full-time employee receives 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave must be requested at least 7 days in advance through the HR portal and approved by the line manager.

Unused annual leave can be carried forward to the next year, up to a maximum of 5 days. Any leave above 5 days lapses on 31 December.

## Sick leave
Employees get 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required when sick leave lasts more than 2 consecutive days.

## Maternity and paternity leave
Maternity leave is 26 weeks of paid leave. Paternity leave is 10 working days of paid leave and must be taken within 3 months of the birth.
