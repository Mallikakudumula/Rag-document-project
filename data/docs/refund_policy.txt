Acme Retail Refund and Returns Policy

Customers can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be unused and in the original packaging with the receipt or order number.

Electronics have a shorter return window of 14 days. Opened software and gift cards cannot be returned.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the returned item is received and inspected. Shipping fees are not refunded unless the item was damaged or incorrect.

To start a return, contact support at returns@acme.example with your order number.
